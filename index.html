Tee sheet reference

Choose a screenshot from Photos or Files. The image is displayed only in this browser session; it is not uploaded, saved to local storage, or included in CSV exports.

The sample club screenshot uses separate rows for tee 1 and tee 2 at the same time. The number 1 or 2 is a starting tee identifier, not a Red/Blue/Gold course selection. Choose the correct 18-hole routing for each group.

Import tee slots from screenshot

Enter the tee time and starting tee number from the screenshot. Player names and handicaps are not needed. Automatic image-to-text recognition is not included in this offline version.

Existing groups are not overwritten. Duplicate tee times on different starting tees are supported. Review the course routing before importing.

Data and controls

Data stays in this browser's local storage. It is not synchronised across devices and may be lost if browser data is cleared.

Pace status is based on observed positions, not GPS. A group may be behind its expected hole without a confirmed delay; only an actual observation can establish a minimum delay.

Course assumptions

Six routes, 18 holes each, 240-minute round target. Reverse courses swap the order of nine-hole loops rather than playing holes backwards. Hole allowances include walking time.

These timings are from the earlier golf calculator and should be validated against actual course conditions.

Same-day timetable estimates only. Expected position includes the walk to the next tee. Minimum delay compares the check time with the scheduled end of the observed hole. A zero-minute delay does not establish that the group is on pace. Confirm congestion ahead before intervening.
